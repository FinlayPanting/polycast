The basic idea was really simple.

Mia steps in at the next pause

The host is real. Your guide only speaks in the pauses.

Mia or Leo pick their moments: a word from the host’s own sentence, the story behind a reference, a quick question to check you’re following.

From a show you love to words that stick

Every episode is prepared once, before you listen, so nothing interrupts the host.

As much help as you want, and no more

Choose how often your guide steps in. Change it whenever you like, episode to episode.

  • Hands-onMore moments and more words. For when the language is new.
  • StandardA steady rhythm of words and recaps through the episode.
  • LightThe occasional nudge. Mostly just you and the host.
  • Guide offPlain subtitles, for when you’re nearly there.

Made in Salzburg, built in the open

Polycast is made by Finlay Panting, who learnt his languages by clipping lines from the podcasts he loved. It’s built for learning for fun, not homework: pick a show you’d enjoy anyway, put your headphones in, and let the language wash over you.

The whole app is open on GitHub, if you’d like to see how it works.
