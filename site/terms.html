What Polycast is

Polycast helps you learn a language by listening to real podcasts with subtitles, translations and a spoken guide. Episodes come from each show's own public feed. Polycast doesn't own the shows and isn't connected to the people who make them, unless it says so.

Free and Plus

  • Free: every episode that's already been prepared, with subtitles, plus the guide on one episode a week. Early accounts may also get one free episode of their own choosing to prepare.
  • Plus: the guide on every episode, your whole word bank with spaced reviews, and up to 5 hours of new episodes a month. €4.99 a month or €49 a year, or the equivalent shown in the App Store in your country.

Plus is bought through the App Store and renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You can manage or cancel it in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Payment, refunds and any consumer rights to withdraw from the purchase are handled by Apple under its terms. Unused hours don't carry over to the next month.
