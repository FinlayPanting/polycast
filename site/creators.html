For podcast creators

Polycast helps people learn a language by listening to shows like yours. Here's how it treats your work.

How your show appears

  • Your audio comes from your feed. Every listen downloads the episode from your own feed, so it counts in your hosting stats, and your adverts stay in. Polycast never stores or re-hosts your audio.
  • Your name and artwork stay on it. Episodes show your show's title, artwork and author, as they appear in your feed.
  • Learners hear you, not a copy. Polycast adds subtitles and translations, and a guide who only speaks when you pause. Nothing in your episode is changed or cut.
  • New listeners. People learning your language find shows they'd never have come across, and often keep listening once they can follow along.

Ask for your show to be removed

If you'd rather your show wasn't on Polycast, tell us here. It'll be taken off within a few days, and you'll get a reply to confirm.

Prefer email? Write to hello@polycast.app. The same address is there for anything else, from partnerships to featuring your show.
